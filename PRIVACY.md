# Privacy Policy — Stock Average Down Calculator

**Last updated**: March 16, 2026

## Data Collection

This extension does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All calculations are performed locally in your browser.

## Local Storage

The extension uses Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API solely to save your input values (average price, current price, shares owned) and preferences (language setting) so they persist between sessions. This data never leaves your device.

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.
